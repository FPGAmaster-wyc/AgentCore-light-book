Four steps to set up

Steps 1 and 4 are the same everywhere; steps 2 and 3 follow the system you are using.

  1. 1

    Press and hold to power on Same on every system

    Press and hold the button on the device for about 3 seconds, then let go. The three lights flash briefly and then settle on red blinking — the device is on and waiting to connect.

    Plugging in the USB cable does not power it on; you still press and hold. Press and hold for 3 seconds to power off as well.

  2. 2

    Install the app Follows your system

    1. Download AgentCore-Light.zip above and unzip it to get AgentCore-Light.exe.
    2. Double-click AgentCore-Light.exe to run it. The app opens a browser page — that is the console.

    Windows says “Windows protected your PC” — what now?

    Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. The app has no paid code-signing certificate and new releases have few downloads, so SmartScreen blocking it is normal — it is not a virus. If your antivirus warns or quarantines the file, add it to the trusted list.

    1. Download AgentCore-Light-mac.zip above and unzip it to get AgentCore-Light.app.
    2. Drag AgentCore-Light.app into your Applications folder. Do not skip this — running it straight from the unzipped folder breaks start-at-sign-in, AI tool connections and online updates later on.
    3. Find it in Applications, Control-click (or right-click) the icon, choose “Open”, and confirm once more.

    Mac says it “cannot verify the developer” — what now?

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, find the message that blocked it, and click Open Anyway. From macOS 15 on, Control-click no longer works — this is the only way.

    If it says “is damaged and can’t be opened”, open Terminal, run the line below, and try opening it again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AgentCore-Light.app

    If double-clicking does nothing at all, the app is usually not in your Applications folder — go back to step 2 above.

    1. Download AgentCore-Light-linux.tar.gz above and unzip it.
    2. Open a terminal and change into the unzipped folder.
    3. The first run needs execute permission; then start it:

    chmod +x AgentCore-Light ./AgentCore-Light

    Do not start it with sudo ./AgentCore-Light.

    The app runs as an ordinary user throughout — it neither needs nor should have elevated rights. Only the serial-permission command in step 3 below needs sudo.

  3. 3

    Connect to your computer Follows your system

    After powering on, the device goes into Bluetooth mode by itself, with the status light red blinking to show it is waiting for the computer. Once connected, red turns into green breathing (the light slowly fading up and down).

    Pick either of the two connection methods below: Bluetooth needs no cable and suits everyday use; USB is more stable when Bluetooth is inconvenient.

    Bluetooth Recommended

    1. Open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth, wait for AgentCore-Light to appear in the list and select it.
    2. Open its device details page and click “Connect” once more. Pairing alone is not enough — this step is required.
    3. Go back to the app and click Start Bluetooth mode.

    Windows may show it as an audio device, under a different name — that is so Windows manages the connection reliably. It will not play your computer’s sound.

    1. Click Start Bluetooth mode in the app; it searches and connects by itself.
    2. No need to add anything by hand in the system Bluetooth settings.
    1. Click Start Bluetooth mode in the app; it searches and connects by itself.
    2. No need to add anything by hand in the system Bluetooth settings. If your computer has no Bluetooth, use the USB method on the right.

    USB cable When Bluetooth is awkward

    1. Short-press the button once on the device to switch to USB serial mode (yellow blinking).
    2. Connect it to the computer with a Type-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge and the device will not be recognised — try another one.
    3. Click Start USB mode in the app.

    The first time you use USB, grant your account serial port permission, then log out and back in (just closing the terminal window is not enough):

    sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER

    Connected looks like this: green breathing on the device (slowly fading up and down) and the app showing the device as connected. The two connections cannot be on at once; short-press the button to switch.

  4. 4

    Test the lights and buzzer Same on every system

    1. When the app starts it opens a browser page — that is the console. If it does not open by itself, go to http://127.0.0.1:18793/.
    2. Under Light control, click the red, yellow and green lights in turn and confirm the device lights up with each one.
    3. Click the buzzer and confirm you hear a tone.

    Once the lights and the buzzer both work, the device is working correctly and you can start using it.

    All three work — now you can attach the magnetic plate. If any of them does not, do not attach it yet; contact us first. Once the backing is peeled off the magnetic plate and it is attached, the item can no longer be returned under our no-questions-asked policy.

Two ways to mount it

Once all four steps check out, decide where the device should live. It can hang on the side of your monitor or stand on the desk — a magnetic plate is included in the box.

Side mount · hang it on your monitor

Stick the magnetic plate to the side of your monitor and the device snaps onto it, hanging at the edge of the screen. It takes up no desk space, and one glance up tells you the current status.

Base mount · stand it on the desk

Stick the magnetic plate to the desk and the device snaps down onto it, standing upright. You can place it wherever suits you, and it is harder to knock over.

  1. Confirm the device works: if you have not finished the light and buzzer test in step 4, do that first.
  2. Wipe the device clean: if the shell has dust or fingerprints, wipe it once with the alcohol pad in the box.
  3. Attach the magnetic plate: peel off the backing and press it firmly where you chose — the side of the monitor or the desk.
  4. Snap the device on: bring the device onto the plate and give it a light push to confirm it holds.

What the status lights mean

Worth coming back to from time to time. The red, yellow and green lights always mean the same things.

Before any AI tool is connected

All LEDs flash

Powering on — one moment.

Red blinking

Bluetooth mode, waiting for the computer to connect.

Yellow blinking

USB serial mode, waiting for the app to connect.

Green breathing

Connected and idle. Green fading slowly up and down means the connection is good.

All LEDs off

Powered off. Press and hold the button for 3 seconds to power on again.

Once your AI tools take over

Idle IDLE

Nothing is running — green breathing at low brightness. After 10 minutes continuously idle it goes dark to sleep, and lights up again by itself when new work arrives.

Working BUSY

A task is running — yellow slow blink (about once a second).

Waiting WAIT_CONFIRM

The AI needs you to authorise or confirm something — yellow steady.

Error ERROR

The task failed or hit an error — red fast blink.

Done SUCCESS

The task succeeded — green breathing plus one “rising” tone, then back to idle after 5 seconds. The tone is what confirms the task is done.

Rising tone

One button, three ways to press it

The body has a single button; a short press and a long press do different things.

Short press

Switches between Bluetooth mode (red blinking) and USB mode (yellow blinking). Only one connection can be on at a time — a short press switches it.

Hold 3 seconds

Power on and power off are both a 3-second hold. Powered off, every status light goes out and it uses the least battery; plugging in USB will not power it on either — you still hold the button.

Type-C charging

Charge over Type-C, and you can use it while it charges. Battery level, voltage and whether it is charging are all visible in the console.

Stuck?

These cover almost everything. Click one to open it.

It keeps blinking red and will not connect

Red blinking means the device is fine and waiting for the computer. The problem is usually on the computer side:

  1. Windows: clicking “Start Bluetooth mode” in the app is not enough. Pair it first under Settings → Bluetooth & devices, then open the device details page and click “Connect” once, and only then start Bluetooth mode in the app.
  2. Check that the device is not already connected to another computer or phone — Bluetooth connects to only one device at a time.
  3. If it still will not connect, switch to USB: short-press the button once for yellow blinking, plug in a data cable, and click Start USB mode in the app.
It keeps blinking yellow and will not connect

Yellow blinking means the device has switched to USB mode and is waiting for the app. Check each of these:

  • Have you clicked Start USB mode in the app?
  • Is the cable a charge-only one? Swap in a Type-C cable that carries data.
  • Is the device really in USB mode? A short press of the button switches between the two modes.
  • Linux: have you granted serial port permission? It only takes effect after you log out and back in — closing the terminal window is not enough.
The app says it is connected but the light does not change
  • First click the red light by hand under Light control in the console. If it lights up normally, the connection is fine.
  • Check that brightness has not been set to 0%.
  • Once your AI tools take over, the light follows the AI’s state. Idle is green breathing at low brightness — dim is normal.
The light goes out by itself after a while

That is normal, not a fault. Once your AI tools take over, 10 minutes continuously idle (the default) turns all status lights off and puts the device to sleep to save battery. The connection is not dropped — the light comes back by itself when the AI has new work, with no button press needed.

To change the time or turn the feature off, see chapter 8 of the full manual.

The device powered itself off

Also normal. In Bluetooth mode, 5 minutes with no connection (computer shut down, Bluetooth off, or the app quit) makes the device power itself off to save battery — even while USB charging is plugged in.

Press and hold the button for 3 seconds to power on again. That time can be changed or turned off too; see the full manual.

Windows or my antivirus says this software is a virus

The app is made by an independent developer and has no paid code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen, macOS Gatekeeper or your antivirus may block it the first time — that is the missing signature, not a virus inside the software.

Each system’s prompts and how to get past them have their own section at the start of the full manual; just follow the steps.
